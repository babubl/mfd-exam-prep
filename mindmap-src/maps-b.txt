@ch4|4|10|Legal and Regulatory Framework|Regulators, SEBI reforms, investment restrictions, the advertisement code, investor rights, grievances and the MFD code of conduct.
Legal and Regulatory Framework
  Regulators
    RBI: banks, money market
    SEBI: securities (regulates MFs)
    IRDAI: insurance
    PFRDA: pension
    All under the Ministry of Finance
    AMFI is not a regulator
    RBI rules also apply: money market, overseas investment, forex
  SEBI
    Preamble: protect investors, develop and regulate the market
    MF Regulations 1996 → replaced by MF Regulations 2026
    "Securities" defined in Sec 2(h), SCRA 1956
    Reforms
      Benchmark = Total Return Index (TRI), not Price Return Index
      Debt PRC matrix: Macaulay duration × Credit Risk Value (1 Dec 2021)
      Segregated portfolio after the 2018 credit crisis
      2008 crisis: no premature FMP redemption; "liquid plus" name banned
  Investment restrictions
    Regulation 39: permitted investments
      Securities, money market instruments
      Privately placed debentures
      Securitised debt (asset / mortgage backed)
      Gold, silver; anything else SEBI specifies
    Regulation 41
      Follow the Sixth Schedule (except gold / silver ETFs)
      Settle only in demat
      No carry-forward deals
      Derivatives and short selling only on a recognised exchange
      Inter-scheme transfers only on SEBI's conditions
    Sixth Schedule
      Equity and debt only if listed or to be listed
      Except unlisted G-secs, money market (not CPs), unlisted NCDs as allowed
      Unrated debt on SEBI's conditions
    Fund of funds rules
      Scheme may invest in another MF scheme with no fees
      No scheme can invest in a FoF
      FoF can't invest in another FoF; holds only MF units
    Gold / silver ETFs
      Only gold / silver or related instruments
      May use exchange-traded commodity derivatives
      Idle money in short-term bank deposits
    Delivery basis only; securities held in the MF's name
    Idle money: short-term deposits of scheduled commercial banks
    SIF (Regulation 51): same instruments as Reg 39 + Sixth Schedule
  Advertisement code
    Fifth Schedule of MF Regulations 2026
    Banned
      Testimonials, rankings, celebrities, projections
      Unfair comparisons
    Standard warning
      "Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully."
      No words added or removed
      Audio-visual: 14 words, visual + voice-over, ≥5 seconds
      Vernacular ad: warning in the vernacular
    Performance ads
      <6 months: no performance
      6–12 months: simple annualised 6-month return
      >3 years: CAGR 1, 3, 5 yrs + since inception + Rs 10,000 point-to-point
      Say regular or direct
      Overnight, liquid, money market: simple annualised 7, 15, 30 days
      Same fund manager's schemes; >6 = count + top 3 and bottom 3
    Additional benchmark
      Equity, balanced, aggressive, BAF, multi-asset, retirement, children's: Sensex / Nifty
      Debt ≤1 yr and arbitrage: 1-yr T-bill
      Other debt, conservative hybrid, equity savings: 10-yr GoI
    IDCW ads
      Rs per unit + face value + prevailing NAV
      Just below: NAV falls by payout and statutory levy
    Celebrity endorsement
      Industry level only, prior SEBI approval
      Paid from investor education pool; never for a scheme or AMC brand
    SIF branding
      Distinct brand, logo, website / page
      MF brand for 5 yrs: "brought to you by"; MF font no bigger
    Unverified market news: only after Compliance Officer approval
  Investor rights
    Ownership
      Proportionate beneficial ownership
      Unit certificate or demat; demat freely transferable (except ELSS lock-in)
      Can pledge units
      Up to 10 nominees; no % = equal split
    Switch distributor or go direct: written request, no NOC
    Can inspect: Trust Deed, IMA, Custodial and RTA agreements, AMC Memorandum & Articles
    Fundamental attribute change
      Written communication (email / SMS) + AMC website
      Exit at NAV, no load, ≥30 days
    75% of unitholders can remove the AMC or wind up a scheme
    Sue the trustees, not the trust (notional entity)
    Caveat emptor; prospective investor has no such rights
    Unclaimed money
      Within 3 yrs: prevailing NAV
      After 3 yrs: NAV at end of year 3
      Separate liquid plan; max fee 0.50% p.a.; no exit load
    Debt trades disclosed daily, 15-day lag
    Recovered illiquid security: within 2 yrs to old investors, else Investor Education Fund
    Complaint status: annual report, MF site, AMFI site
  Grievances and codes
    AMC resolves complaints in 21 calendar days
    SCORES: SEBI's online system; physical complaints scanned and uploaded
    Cyber framework CSCRF (CERT-In): Anticipate, Withstand, Contain, Recover, Evolve
    Fourth Schedule: SEBI code for AMCs and trustees
    AMFI Code of Ethics (ACE): higher, more explicit standards
    AGNI: AMFI Guidelines & Norms for Intermediaries
    Breach process
      Explanation within 3 weeks
      Warning letter
      2nd proven violation: ARN cancelled, all AMCs told
      Appeal to AMFI allowed
  MFD code (Appendix 3)
    Fiduciary: investor's interest is paramount
    Never
      Rebates or pass-back, gifts, inducements
      Churning or splitting applications
      Indicative portfolio, yield or assured returns
      Own contact details on a client form
      Deal in direct plans
      Share client data with group companies for cross-selling
      Call yourself adviser, wealth manager, consultant (unless SEBI RIA)
    Always
      Disclose all commissions and group conflicts
      Add the EUIN
      Use AMC-provided material; logo only with written approval
      Check suitability: financial status, experience, objectives
      Sub-distributors hold a valid ARN
      Refund clawback commissions
      Report moral turpitude or arrest to AMC and AMFI
      Tagline "AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor", font ≥12

@ch5|5|10|Scheme Related Information|SID, SAI and KIM, fundamental attributes, update deadlines and every disclosure: NAV, TER, risk-o-meter, portfolio, results and reports.
Scheme Related Information
  Mandatory documents
    Scheme related documents = SID + SAI (+ KIM)
    Caveat emptor: investor presumed to have read them
    SEBI format, same sequence; SEBI vets with observations, never approves
    Drafts on SEBI site; finals on AMFI and MF sites
    Contract: offer = documents; acceptance = form + payment; completes on allotment
  SID
    Details of one scheme
    Cover page: name, type, face value, NFO dates, AMC and trustee
    Disclaimer: AMC Due Diligence Certificate; SEBI hasn't approved it
    Risk-o-meter + suitability on front page
    Standard risks: sponsor not liable beyond initial contribution
    Eligible investors: NFO and Ongoing Offer section
    Objective can be primary + secondary
  SAI
    Statutory info on the MF / AMC; one SAI for all schemes
    Legally part of the SID
    Constituents, condensed financials (last 3 FYs), valuation norms
    How to apply, modes of transaction, nomination, pledge, tax
    Printed copy on request; all SAIs on AMFI site
  KIM and addendum
    KIM = summary of SID + SAI
    Every application form must carry a KIM
    Performance 1, 3, 5 yrs and since inception
    Addendum = interim change; part of the documents; goes with KIM
  Fundamental attributes
    Type of scheme
    Investment objective + min / max asset allocation
    Terms of issue: liquidity, fees and expenses, safety net
    To change (Reg 22(9)(c))
      Written communication (email / SMS) to each unitholder
      Details on AMC website
      Exit at prevailing NAV, no exit load
  Update timelines
    SID: within 6 months of launch half-year; then 1 month after each half-year
    Fundamental change: addendum now; SID revised after ≥30-day exit window
    Other changes: addendum to website, distributors and ISCs
    SAI: within 3 months of FY end
    KIM: every half-year, within 1 month; filed electronically
  NAV and expense disclosure
    NAV every business day on AMC and AMFI sites
    Liquid and overnight: also Sundays and holidays
    Close-ended: at least weekly
    TER daily (except IDFs), Format 7E, machine-readable
    TER = BER + brokerage + transaction cost + statutory levies
    BER under Regulation 66(7); direct plan BER lower
    BER change: email / SMS + website ≥3 working days before
    Expense item >10% of total disclosed in accounts
    Scheme dashboard: AUM, TER, manager, benchmark, exit load, portfolio
  Risk-o-meter
    6 levels
      Low: Irish Green
      Low to Moderate: Chartreuse
      Moderate: Neon Yellow
      Moderately High: Caramel
      High: Dark Orange
      Very High: Red
    Reviewed monthly; change = notice-cum-addendum + email / SMS
    Scheme and benchmark risk-o-meter shown together
    Commodity score (15-yr annualised volatility, quarterly)
      <10% = 3
      10–15% = 4
      15–20% = 5
      >20% = 6
    Only a first step, not the sole basis for decisions
    SIF Risk-Band: 5 levels, set at NFO, monthly, published in 10 days
  Portfolio and reports
    Portfolio: monthly and half-yearly, with ISIN, within 10 days
    Debt schemes: fortnightly, within 5 days, plus yield
    SIF: every alternate month, within 10 days; ISID has scenario analysis
    Unaudited half-yearly results: within 1 month, email / SMS to unitholders
    Annual report: within 4 months; ad in English + Hindi dailies
    Factsheet: voluntary; monthly is AMFI best practice
    Derivatives only to hedge, no open positions

@ch6|6|6|Fund Distribution and Channel Management|Channels, transaction platforms, becoming a distributor, trail commission, disclosure and due diligence, nomination and change of distributor.
Fund Distribution and Channel Management
  Channels
    Distributor analyses the investor; fund manager analyses the market
    Individuals, banks, national / regional distributors, NBFCs, brokers, post offices, direct, online
    LIC and UTI built the individual agent force
    Employee model = fixed cost; sub-agent model = variable cost
    Multinational banks entered first; PSU banks give non-urban reach
    Distribution companies and banks = institutional distributors
  Platforms
    Paper forms: OPOAs
    Stock exchanges: NSE NMF-II, BSE StAR MF
      AMFI-registered trading members signed up with the MF
      Limited purpose membership for distributors
      DPs can only process redemptions
      Order routing only: don't replace RTAs; AMC settles
      Investors can use directly (26 Feb 2020)
      SIP, STP, SWP, switch; units to demat
    MF Utilities
      CAN needs KYC; investor may hold several
      One form, one payment, many schemes
      MFU bank / nomination overrides folio
    RTA interoperable platform: since 31 Dec 2021
    MFCentral (CAMS + KFintech)
      Consolidated view of demat + non-demat folios
      Service requests and transactions
      CAS, unclaimed amounts, family portfolio
    New-age apps (Groww, Kuvera, Paytm Money, Coin): direct plans too
  Becoming a distributor
    NISM V-A
      CPE exemption: age ≥50 or ≥10 yrs experience on 31 May 2010
    KYD: PAN + address proof at CAMS PoS, right index finger
    New cadre (V-B) exempt from KYD biometrics
    ARN: minimum age 18; online only from 1 Oct 2024
    Employees: EUIN under firm's ARN
    Empanelment: compulsory, no standard form, AMC can end anytime
  Commission
    Full trail only (Master Circular 20 Mar 2026)
      No upfront; no upfronting of trail; monetary only
      AMC pays (Regulation 66(6))
    Trail on daily net assets, paid monthly or quarterly
    Daily trail = AUM × rate ÷ 365 (Rs 10 lakh at 1% = Rs 27.40)
    No commission on self-investment
    Transaction charges withdrawn (Aug 2025)
    Additional commission (Nov 2025)
      New PAN from B-30, or new women investors (Top 30 + B-30)
      Lump sum: 1%, max Rs 2,000, if held ≥1 yr
      SIP: 1% of first year, max Rs 2,000
      From 2 bps investor education pool; clawback; no dual incentive
    GST
      From July 2017; threshold Rs 20 lakh
      Unregistered distributor: recipient pays (reverse charge)
  Disclosure and due diligence
    Trigger (any one)
      >20 locations
      >Rs 100 cr AUM (non-institutional)
      >Rs 1 cr commission a year
      >Rs 50 lakh from one AMC
    Yearly: inflows, average AUM, AUM / gross inflows, sponsor associate?
    Turnover >2× industry average: extra due diligence
    Checks: business model, penalties, associates, suitability separate from sales
  Advisory vs execution only
    Advice is incidental to distribution: no RIA needed
    Can't call himself adviser or planner
    Only two types: advisory or execution only
    Unsuitable + execution only: written warning + written confirmation
    Mis-selling under PFUTP 2003
  Distributor nomination
    Commission to nominee until ARN change or AUM nil
    Nominee needs no ARN; receives as trustee for legal heirs
    ARN valid on date of death; no new business or SIPs
    AUM transfer: valid ARN + KYD; clients told; 15 days to object
  Change of distributor
    Investor change: no commission to either (unless old ceased business)
    New distributor may get trail after 12-month cooling-off
    Distributor-initiated only for
      Name / legal status change
      Merger / acquisition within group
      Family AUM consolidation
      Transfer of business
      Entire AUM, valid KYD ARN, old ARN surrendered

@ch7|7|8|NAV, TER and Pricing of Units|Valuation principles, the NAV formula, every TER slab, AT1/AT2 limits, IDCW rules, loads and segregated portfolios.
NAV, TER and Pricing of Units
  Valuation
    Seventh Schedule; aim = fair to entering, exiting, staying investors
    10 principles; they prevail over the Guidelines
    Principle 1: realisable value, in good faith
    Policy
      Approved by AMC Board; new security type needs a method first
      Independent auditor review at least yearly
      Disclosed in SAI and website
      AMC responsible; may deviate (report to Boards, tell investors)
    Traded equity: last close on principal exchange
    Not traded today: other exchange or previous day within 30 days
    Non-traded (no trade 30 days)
      Good faith, AMC Board methods; trustees review
      Earnings capitalisation ± book value, less illiquidity discount
    Convertibles: debt and equity parts separately
    Warrants: share value − exercise price − discount
    Debt: trade prices from all public platforms
    Gold ETF: LBMA AM fixing, 995.0 fineness
    Silver ETF: LBMA AM fixing, 999.0 fineness
  NAV
    NAV = net assets ÷ units outstanding
    Net assets = total assets − liabilities other than to unitholders
    Example: Rs 217 cr ÷ 20 cr units = Rs 10.85
    Includes unrealised gains
    Accrual principle: count when due, not when paid
    Short-term borrowing for liquidity ≤6 months
    Mark to market stops dilution
      Issue below NAV lowers NAV for existing investors
      Redeem below NAV raises it
  Expenses and TER
    All expenses paid from the scheme, within base limits
    Allowed: distribution, brokerage, RTA, trustee, audit, custodian, insurance, listing, storage of gold / silver
    Above limits: borne by AMC, trustees or sponsor
    Equity-oriented for TER: ≥65% equity
    Open-ended slabs (equity / other)
      First Rs 500 cr: 2.10 / 1.85
      Next 250: 1.90 / 1.65
      Next 1,250: 1.60 / 1.40
      Next 3,000: 1.50 / 1.25
      Next 5,000: 1.40 / 1.15
      Next 40,000: −0.05% per Rs 5,000 cr
      Balance: 0.95 / 0.70
    Index fund / ETF: ≤0.90%
    FoF
      Into liquid / index / ETF: 0.90%
      ≥65% in equity schemes: 2.10%
      Others: 1.85%
      Own layer ≤2× underlying TER
    Close-ended / interval: equity 1.00%, others 0.80%
    Brokerage on top: ≤0.06% cash, ≤0.02% derivatives
    Total = base + brokerage + transaction cost + levies; nothing else but exit load
    Old extra 0.05% for exit-load schemes: gone
  AT1 and AT2 bonds
    MF ≤10% of one issuer's bonds
    Scheme ≤10% of assets; ≤5% in one issuer
    AT1 deemed maturity: 100 yrs from issue
    AT2: contractual maturity
    AT1 valued on yield to call
    Close-ended debt schemes can't buy perpetuals
  IDCW
    Only from distributable surplus
      Valuation gains ignored
      Valuation losses deducted
    Trustees decide amount and record date
    Monthly-or-more payouts can be delegated to AMC officials
    Notice within 1 day; record date 5 calendar days later
    No notice for daily-to-monthly payouts in liquid / debt
    Late payment: interest from record date
    May include capital (equalisation reserve); split disclosed
  Loads and pricing
    Entry load banned: sale price = NAV (+ stamp duty)
    Exit load: price = NAV × (1 − load)
      Rs 11 with 1% load = Rs 10.89
    No distinction by amount; changes only prospective
    None on bonus or IDCW-reinvestment units
    Credited back to the scheme
    Rs 25,000 at NAV 43.21 = 578.570 units
  Accounting
    Scheme accounts separate from AMC, different auditors
    NAV decimals: 4 for index, liquid, debt; ≥2 for equity, balanced
    Fractional units allowed; exchanges trade whole units
    Segregated portfolio
      No management fee
      Other TER only pro-rata on recovery
      NAV declared daily
      Unrated debt: only on actual default

@ch8|8|4|Taxation|What decides the tax, the rate table, grandfathering, IDCW, stamp duty and STT, loss set-off, ELSS, TDS and GST.
Taxation
  Scheme and options
    Scheme income exempt: Sec 10(23D), MFs are trusts
    Growth: capital gains only
    IDCW: dividend + capital gains
    Joint holding: taxed in first holder's hands
  What decides the tax
    Type of income, holding period
    Type of scheme, type of investor
    Purchase date (1 Apr 2023) and redemption date (23 Jul 2024)
    Equity-oriented for tax: >65% listed Indian equity
    FoF in equity MFs = non-equity
    Holding periods: 12 months (listed, equity funds) or 24 months
  Tax rates (STCG / LTCG)
    Equity-oriented (incl. hybrid >65% equity)
      20% / 12.5% after 12 months
      Rs 1.25 lakh LTCG exempt per FY
    Debt-oriented (hybrid >65% debt): slab rate
    Hybrid 35–65% equity: slab / 12.5% after 24 months
    Other MFs (FoFs, international): slab / 12.5% after 24 months
    Equity and Gold ETFs: slab / 12.5% after 12 months (as printed)
    Debt ETFs: slab rate
    Exemption examples
      Rs 90,000 gain: no tax
      Rs 1.35 lakh gain: tax on Rs 10,000
    Grandfathering
      Gains up to 31 Jan 2018 exempt
      Cost = higher of actual cost or 31 Jan 2018 NAV
      Cost 1 lakh, Jan-18 value 2 lakh: sold 1.8 lakh = 0; 2.2 lakh = Rs 20,000
  IDCW
    DDT abolished (Budget Feb 2020)
    From April 2020: taxed at investor's slab
    Tax-exempt investors pay nil
    IDCW cuts NAV by more than the post-tax receipt
    Growth more tax-efficient: deferral
    From 1 Apr 2021: split into income and capital distribution
  Stamp duty and STT
    Stamp duty (1 Jul 2020)
      0.005% on issue: purchase, SIP, STP, switch-in, reinvestment
      0.015% on transfer
      Units allotted after stamp duty
    STT
      Only equity-oriented funds, on sale / redemption
      None on purchase; none on debt funds
      Redemption to MF: 0.001%
      Exchange delivery sale: 0.001%
      Non-delivery: 0.025%
      Paid by the seller
  Losses
    No set-off against salary or other heads
    STCL against STCG or LTCG
    LTCL only against LTCG
    Carry forward up to 8 years
    Bonus stripping
      Bought ≤3 months before record date, sold ≤9 months after
      Loss disallowed; becomes cost of bonus units
  ELSS (Tax Saver)
    80C up to Rs 1.5 lakh (individual / HUF)
    Old Tax Regime only
    3-year lock-in from each SIP and each reinvestment
    Benefit to the first holder
    New regime: just an equity fund with lock-in
    Old retirement funds: 80C with 5-year lock-in
  TDS
    No TDS on redemption for residents
    IDCW to residents: 10% if > Rs 10,000 a year
    NRIs: lower of Income Tax Act or DTAA rate
  GST and expenses
    GST on management fee: over and above TER
    GST on other fees, brokerage: within TER
    From 1 Apr 2026: BER + brokerage + statutory levies
    GST on exit load deducted from the load
    GST on distributor commission can't hit the scheme
