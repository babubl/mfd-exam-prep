Mutual Fund (a Trust)
  Legal base
    SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026
    Registration granted under Regulation 3
    Indian Trusts Act, 1882 governs the trust
    Trust deed registered under Indian Registration Act, 1908
    MF = trust raising money by selling units, under one or more schemes
      Invests in securities, money market, gold, silver and other assets SEBI allows
  Regulators
    SEBI
      Registers the mutual fund
      Prior approval: trustees, AMC, change in AMC control
      Gives observations on SID/SAI (vets, never approves)
      Complaints via SCORES
    RBI
      Money market, forex, overseas investment rules
    Stock exchanges
      Listing, trading and margin rules for listed units
  Sponsor
    Creates the trust; main person behind the MF
    Applies to SEBI for registration
    Invests in the AMC's capital
    Eligibility
      ≥ 5 years in financial services
      Positive net worth in each of last 5 years
      Net profit in each of last 5 years
      Average net profit ≥ Rs 10 crore (5 yrs)
      Liquid net worth > capital contribution to AMC
      Fit and proper; sound track record
    Must keep AMC net worth ≥ Rs 50 crore at all times
    Liability limited to initial contribution
    Categories (AMFI): banks, institutions, private sector (Indian, foreign, JV)
  Trustees
    Form
      Individuals = Board of Trustees
      Or a Trustee Company (acts via its Board of Directors)
    Composition
      At least 2/3 independent directors
      Independent = not associate of sponsor, AMC or trustee company
      Prior SEBI approval to appoint
    Eligibility (Regulation 11)
      Fit and proper (Schedule II, Intermediaries Regulations 2008)
      Not a director, officer or employee of an AMC
      Not a trustee of another mutual fund
      Gives SEBI any information it asks for
    Duties (6 heads)
      Appoint and terminate the AMC
      Conflicts of interest and broker empanelment
      Regulatory compliance
      Oversight for investor interests
      Additional oversight
      Due diligence on matters SEBI specifies
    Sign Investment Management Agreement with AMC
    Appoint custodian and scheme auditor
    Can remove AMC by majority (with SEBI + unitholder approval)
  Asset Management Company (AMC)
    Appointment and rules
      Appointed by sponsor or trustees, with SEBI approval
      Net worth ≥ Rs 50 crore (from 1 Jan 2024)
      ≥ 50% directors not associates of sponsor or trustees
      Directors: finance experience; fit and proper
      Director appointment needs trustee approval
      AMC directors (incl. Chairman) can't sit on any MF trustee board
      No one can be a director of more than one AMC
      Change of control: trustees + SEBI approval
        Letter to each unitholder
        Ads: English national daily + regional language
        Exit at NAV, no load, ≥ 30 days
      Removal: majority of trustees or 75% of unitholders
    Fund management
      Analysts: find opportunities
      Fund managers: decide; own scheme performance
      Dealers: place orders with brokers
    Compliance Officer
      Signs due diligence certificate on SID
      Reports to AMC head
      Flags non-compliance directly to trustees
    Operations and customer service
      Front office and back office
      RTA liaison: records, allotment, statements
      Custody team: settlement with custodian
      Fund accounting: books and daily NAV
    Risk management (SEBI RMF)
      Investment risk
      Compliance risk
      Operational risk
      Cyber security
    Sales and marketing
      Branding, advertising, events
      Distributor relationships and training
    Support functions
      Finance / Accounts (AMC's own books)
      Administration
      HRD
      IT / technology
  Service providers
    Custodian
      Appointed by trustees
      SEBI (Custodian) Regulations, 1996
      Holds assets; settles trades
      Tracks corporate actions: dividend, bonus, rights
      Not if sponsor holds ≥ 50% voting rights or directors
    Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA)
      Appointed by AMC; optional (can be in-house)
      Must register with SEBI
      Folios, purchases, redemptions, account statements
      Offices act as Investor Service Centres
      CAMS and KFintech (QRTAs; run MITRA)
    Fund accountant
      Calculates NAV
      No SEBI registration needed; in-house or outsourced
    Auditors
      Scheme auditor: appointed by trustees
      AMC auditor: appointed by AMC
      The two must be different
    Collecting bankers and payment aggregators
      Appointed by AMC
      Payment gateways, wallets, payment banks
    KYC Registration Agencies (KRAs)
      SEBI KRA Regulations, 2011
      One KYC across the securities market (PMLA)
      Central KYC Records Registry: CERSAI
    Valuation agencies
      At least 2, for non-traded or thinly traded debt
      CRISIL and ICRA (appointed by AMFI)
      AMCs use their valuation level
    Credit rating agencies
      CRISIL, ICRA, CARE, India Ratings (Fitch)
      Define corporate bond and credit risk funds
      FMP: invest only at proposed rating or better
    Depositories and DPs
      NSDL and CDSL
      DP is like a bank branch; depository like head office
      ETF units must be held in demat
  Distribution and platforms
    Distributors
      NISM Series V-A + ARN from AMFI
      KYD: PAN, address proof, biometrics at CAMS PoS
      Employees: EUIN under the firm's ARN
      New cadre (limited schemes): NISM V-B
      Individuals, banks, NBFCs, brokers, national distributors
      Can be empanelled with many AMCs
    Stock exchange platforms
      BSE StAR MF
      NSE NMF-II
      Order routing only; AMC handles settlement
    MF Utilities (MFU)
      AMFI industry initiative
      One CAN, one form, many AMCs
    Execution Only Platforms (direct plans)
      Category 1: AMFI-registered, agent of AMCs
      Category 2: SEBI stock broker, agent of investor
    AMC websites and apps; RTA interoperable platform
  AMFI
    Association of all registered AMCs
    Issues ARN; can cancel it for code violations
    Code of Ethics (AMCs) and AGNI code (distributors)
    Investor awareness; represents industry to SEBI, RBI, Govt
    Not a regulator, not an SRO
  Unitholders (investors)
    Beneficiaries of the trust
    Own the scheme's assets proportionately
    Can sue trustees, not the trust (notional entity)
    75% can remove the AMC or wind up a scheme
