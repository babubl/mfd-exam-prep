## Chapter 1 – Investment Landscape (8 marks)

- Objective + amount + timeline = **financial goal**.
- Hospitalisation, theft, fire: cover with **emergency fund + insurance**, not investments.
- Pool approach flaw: **horizon unknown**.
- Shalini: Rs 50 lakh at 8% for 10 years = **Rs 1,07,94,625**.
- Rs 30,000 at 6%: **31,800** (1 yr), **53,725** (10 yrs), **96,214** (20 yrs).
- Rs 10,000 at 8%: **14,693** (5), **21,589** (10), **46,610** (20), **1,00,627** (30); 100 units buys **68** after 5 yrs, **46** after 10.
- **Saving precedes investing**; "saving" comes from "safe".
- Top 3 evaluation factors: **safety, liquidity, returns**.
- Exit penalty hits **returns**, not liquidity.
- Four asset classes: **Real estate, Commodities, Equity, Fixed income**.
- Real estate: **location** is key; illiquid; **not divisible**; self-occupied home = **expense**.
- Commodity derivatives aren't investments: **leveraged + short-term**.
- Only **commodities** give no intermittent cash flow; physical gold = no regular income.
- Bond bought at issue, held to maturity: **no capital gain**.
- Sensex: base **100 in 1979**, \~66,000 by July 2023, **\~16% p.a.**
- Only financial form: **equity and bonds**.
- Table 1.1: **PPF, SSY, SCSS, POMIS, endowment/money-back = Fixed Income**; **REITs, InvITs, REMF = Real Estate**; **coins, art, stamps = Others**.
- **Nominal** = before inflation; **real** = after inflation.
- Listed equity is liquid but **unfit for short-term needs**.
- Credit risk causes: borrower's **ability or intention**.
- Own government's bonds = **safest, lowest rate**.
- Pager industry = **industry-specific** risk.
- **Market-wide risk cannot be diversified**; company-specific can.
- Rates up, bond price **down**; **longer maturity = bigger swing**.
- Risk strategies: **avoid, take a position, diversify**; lay investor = **diversify**.
- Credit risk measured by **rating + spreads**; volatility by **variance, SD, beta, modified duration**.
- Dominant emotions: **fear, greed, hope**.
- Rs 5,000 loss hurts more than gain = **loss aversion** (Kahneman & Tversky).
- Decide first, seek proof later = **confirmation bias**.
- "Known devil vs unknown angel" = **familiarity bias**.
- Bull run drives over-allocation = **recency bias**.
- IT employee with only tech stocks = **interest of the investor**.
- Remedy for biases: third opinion from an **RIA or MFD**.
- Risk profile = **need + ability + willingness**; ability = **finances + horizon**; willingness = **psychology**.
- Strategic = **fixed target mix**; tactical/dynamic = **improve risk-adjusted return**, for **seasoned investors with large surplus**.
- Rebalancing = **sell what rose, buy what fell**; 50:50 with A +20%, B −5% on Rs 1,000 each: **sell Rs 125 of A**.
- Passive breach rebalancing: **30 business days**; excludes index funds/ETFs; **not applicable to overnight funds**.
- DIY hidden costs: **time + mistakes**; 2% fee on Rs 10 lakh = **\~Rs 20,000/yr**.
- A mutual fund is a **different way of investing**, not a different product.

## Chapter 2 – Concept and Role of a Mutual Fund (6 marks)

- You invest **through** a mutual fund, not **in** one; it is a **different way of investing**, not a different product.
- A mutual fund is set up as a **trust**.
- Profits and losses belong **only to unitholders**; everyone else earns a **fee or commission**.
- MFs act as **market stabiliser**: in **2022** MF inflows countered heavy **FPI** selling.
- Each separate pool of money = a **scheme**, with a pre-announced **investment objective**.
- Investment policy = **asset allocation + investment style**, disclosed in the **SID**.
- Face value = **Rs 10**; unit capital = **units × face value**.
- AUM = **NAV × units outstanding**; AMCs are ranked by **AUM**.
- Dividend option is now **IDCW** (Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal).
- NAV = **net realisable value per unit** if the scheme were liquidated.
- **Mark to market** = valuing each security at market price, **daily**.
- Recurring expenses are charged as a **% of AUM** and **lower the NAV**.
- Diversification from as little as **Rs 500**.
- Transparency sources: **SID, SAI, KIM + portfolio disclosures + NAV**; available to **prospective investors** too.
- **Tax deferral**: MFs don't pay tax on income; growth option defers tax.
- **ELSS**: deduction up to **Rs 1,50,000** under **Sec 80C**, **Old Tax Regime** only.
- Systematic plans: **SIP** (invest), **SWP** (withdraw), **STP** (transfer).
- Limitations: **no customisation** (PMS gives it), **choice overload**, **no control over costs**, **no guaranteed returns**.
- SEBI **categorisation** was the answer to choice overload.
- MF is a **pass-through vehicle**; the fund manager controls only **his own skill**, not the market or securities.
- **Open-ended**: buy/sell any time after NFO; **unit capital keeps changing**.
- **Close-ended**: buy only in **NFO**; **fixed maturity**; **compulsorily listed** (except **ELSS**); **unit capital stays fixed**.
- Close-ended units usually trade at a **discount to NAV** (buyer has the bargaining power).
- **Interval fund**: close-ended, opens at intervals; **listed**; transaction period **min 2 days**; interval period **15 days** (workbook wording).
- **ETFs** trade all day at multiple prices, need a **demat account**, and track NAV closely; **Silver ETF** now allowed.
- **Active**: higher cost, aims to beat the market. **Passive/index**: tracks index, **low cost**, no fund-manager choice.
- **MF Lite** (Dec 2024): a lighter framework for **passive** schemes only (index funds, ETFs, FoFs).
- SEBI categorisation **2017**: **5 categories**: Equity **11**, Debt **16**, Hybrid **6**, Solution **2**, Other **2**.
- Market cap: **Large 1–100**, **Mid 101–250**, **Small 251+** by **full market cap**.
- Multi cap: equity **≥75%**, with **≥25% each** in large, mid, small.
- Large cap **80%**; Large & Mid **35% + 35%**; Mid cap **65%**; Small cap **65%**.
- Dividend yield **65%**; Value **or** Contra (only one) **65%**.
- Focused: **max 30 stocks**, **65%** equity.
- Sectoral/thematic **80%**; ELSS **80%** + **3-year lock-in**; Flexi cap **65%**, any mix.
- Overnight = **1 day**; can put **≤5%** in G-secs/T-bills (**≤30 days**) for margin.
- Liquid = **up to 91 days**; Money market = **up to 1 year**.
- Macaulay duration: Ultra-short **3–6 m**; Low **6–12 m**; Short **1–3 y**; Medium **3–4 y**; Medium-long **4–7 y**; Long **>7 y**; Dynamic = **any**.
- Corporate bond **80% in AA+ and above**; Credit risk **65% in AA and below** (AA+ excluded).
- Credit-risk scheme names can't stress only return (**no "high yield", "credit opportunities"**).
- Banking & PSU **80%**; Gilt **80%** G-secs; Gilt 10-yr constant duration **80%**, duration **= 10 yrs**; Floater **65%**.
- Conservative hybrid: debt **75–90%**, equity **10–25%**.
- Balanced hybrid: equity **40–60%**, **no arbitrage**. Aggressive hybrid: equity **65–80%**. Offer **only one** of the two.
- Balanced Advantage = **dynamic** equity/debt.
- Multi asset: **≥3 asset classes**, **≥10% each**; **foreign securities are not a separate class**.
- Arbitrage: **65%** equity. Equity savings: **65%** equity + **10%** debt.
- Retirement fund lock-in: **5 years or retirement age, whichever earlier**.
- Children's fund lock-in: **5 years or majority, whichever earlier**.
- Index/ETF **95%** in index securities; FoF **95%** in underlying fund.
- One scheme per category, **except**: index funds/ETFs on different indices, FoFs with different underlyings, sector/thematic on different sectors.
- **FMP**: close-ended debt; portfolio matches maturity; returns clearer but **not guaranteed**.
- **Target Maturity Fund**: matures on a set date (**2–10 years**), **listed**, reduces **reinvestment risk**.
- **Capital protection fund**: close-ended hybrid; e.g. **Rs 90 in debt grows to Rs 100**, rest in **equity derivatives**.
- **IDF**: close-ended, **≥90%** in infra debt; IDF-MF sponsored by **banks and NBFCs**; IDF-NBFC by **banks and IFCs** only.
- **REMF**: **≥35%** in physical assets; **≥75%** in real estate/related; valued every **90 days** by **2 valuers**, **lower value** used; **close-ended, listed**.
- **REITs/InvITs are not MF schemes**. REIT assets **≥Rs 500 cr**, offer **≥Rs 250 cr**; InvIT offer **≥Rs 250 cr**, assets **≥Rs 500 cr**; minimum lot **Rs 10,000–15,000**, traded lot **1 unit**.
- **ESG** sits under **thematic** equity; 6 strategies: exclusion, integration, best-in-class, impact, sustainable objectives, transition.
- **Smart beta**: index-based but **not market-cap weighted** (e.g. equal weight).
- **Quant fund**: model-driven, **removes the human element**.
- **SIF**: fills the gap **between MF and PMS**; minimum **Rs 10 lakh** across strategies.
- SIF equity: Long-Short **≥80%** equity; Ex-Top 100 **≥65%**; Sector Rotation **≥80%**, **max 4 sectors**; redemptions **daily or less often**.
- SIF debt (interval): Sectoral debt **≥2 sectors**, **max 75%** per sector; redemptions **weekly or less often**.
- SIF hybrid (interval): Hybrid Long-Short **≥25% each** in equity and debt; redemptions **twice a week or less often**.
- **Every SIF strategy: max 25% unhedged short exposure**; only **one strategy per category**.
- Industry AUM: **Rs 11.89 lakh cr** (Mar 2015) to **Rs 66.70 lakh cr** (Mar 2025), **18.82% CAGR**.
- Folios: **4.80 cr** (Mar 2010) to **23.45 cr** (Mar 2025).
- Monthly SIP flow: **Rs 8,055 cr** (Mar 2019) to **Rs 26,400 cr** (Jan 2025).
- MF share of household financial investment: **10% → 14%** (2016–18); bank deposits **71% → 65%**.

**Workbook sample answers:** 1-b (NAV), 2-c (economies of scale), 3-c (close-ended).

## Chapter 3 – Legal Structure of Mutual Funds (4 marks)

- Governing regulation: **SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996**.
- MF definition: a fund set up as a **trust**, selling **units** to the public under **schemes**, to invest in securities, money market, **gold, silver, real estate** and other assets SEBI specifies.
- Trust is governed by the **Indian Trusts Act, 1882**; trust deed registered under the **Indian Registration Act, 1908**.
- **Sponsor** creates the trust; **beneficiaries = unitholders**.
- **Trust deed** is executed between **sponsor and trustees**.
- Trustees can be individuals (**Board of Trustees**) or a **trustee company** (acts through its Board of Directors).
- **AMC** is appointed by the **sponsor or trustees**, with **SEBI approval**.
- Trustees sign the **Investment Management Agreement** with the AMC.
- **Custodian** is appointed by the **trustees**; **RTA** by the **AMC**; **collecting banker** by the **AMC**.
- **Sponsor applies** to SEBI for MF registration and invests in the **AMC's capital**.
- Sponsor eligibility: **5 years** in financial services; **positive net worth** in each of the last **5 years**; **net profit** in each of the last **5 years**; **average profit ≥ Rs 10 crore** over 5 years.
- **Liquid net worth** must exceed the sponsor's proposed **capital contribution** to the AMC.
- Trustee bars: **moral turpitude**, **economic offence**, **securities law violation**; no AMC director or employee; no trustee of **another MF**.
- Trustee appointment needs **prior SEBI approval**.
- At least **4 trustees**; at least **two-thirds independent** of the sponsor.
- Trustees review AMC transactions with associates and the **AMC's net worth quarterly**.
- Trustees file **half-yearly reports** to SEBI.
- Trustees must approve any change to **fundamental attributes**, fees or expenses.
- Trustees may take **administrative assistance** to monitor AMCs.
- AMC director appointment needs **prior trustee approval**.
- At least **50%** of AMC directors must be **independent**.
- AMC **net worth ≥ Rs 50 crore** (from **1 Jan 2024**); maintained **continuously**; **sponsor's responsibility**.
- Change of AMC control needs **prior approval of trustees and SEBI**.
- On change of control: letter to each unitholder, ad in **1 English national daily + 1 regional-language paper**, and **exit at NAV with no exit load for ≥30 calendar days**.
- AMC can be removed by **majority of trustees** or **75% of unitholders**; any change needs **SEBI + unitholder approval**.
- Custodian registers under **SEBI (Custodian) Regulations, 1996**.
- Custodian can't be one where the sponsor holds **≥50% voting rights** or **≥50% of directors**, unless conditions are met.
- **Custodian tracks corporate actions** (dividends, bonus, rights) and settles trades.
- **Compliance Officer** signs the **due-diligence certificate** on new scheme documents; reports to the **AMC head**; flags non-compliance **directly to trustees**.
- Fund management sub-teams: **analysts** (find ideas), **fund managers** (own scheme performance), **dealers** (place orders).
- **Fund accounting** team calculates NAV **daily**; the AMC's **accounts** team is separate.
- SEBI **Risk Management Framework**: dedicated risk officers for **investment, compliance, operational, cyber** risk.
- **Fund accountant**: **no SEBI registration** needed; can be in-house.
- **RTA**: **must register with SEBI**, but appointing one is **optional**; its offices act as **ISCs** (Investor Service Centres).
- **Scheme auditor**: appointed by **trustees**, must **differ** from the **AMC auditor** (appointed by the AMC).
- MFD needs **NISM V-A + AMFI registration**; the limited-scheme cadre needs **NISM V-B**.
- **KRAs**: SEBI regulations in **2011**; one KYC across the securities market, under **PMLA**.
- Fair valuation of non-traded debt uses **≥2 valuation agencies**: **CRISIL and ICRA**, appointed by AMFI.
- **FMP**: may disclose **proposed rating profile** before investing; can then invest only **at that rating or safer**.
- Depositories: **NSDL and CDSL**; investors deal through **DPs** (like bank branches).
- **ETF units must be in demat**.
- Exchange platforms: **BSE StAR MF** and **NSE NMF-II**; **MF Utilities** is an **AMFI industry** platform.
- **Execution Only Platform** = only for **direct plans**; RIA and broker platforms for their own clients are **not EOPs**.
- **EOP Category 1**: registers with **AMFI**, body corporate, **agent of AMCs**.
- **EOP Category 2**: registers as a **stock broker** in the exchange EOP segment, **agent of investor**.
- **AMFI** = association of all AMCs; issues **ARN**; can **cancel ARN** for code violations.
- **AMFI is neither a regulator nor an SRO**.

**Workbook sample answers:** 1-c (unitholders), 2-b (custodian).

## Chapter 4 – Legal and Regulatory Framework (10 marks)

### Regulators and SEBI

- 4 regulators: **RBI** (banks, money market), **SEBI** (securities), **IRDAI** (insurance), **PFRDA** (pension); all under **Ministry of Finance**.
- **SEBI regulates mutual funds**; **AMFI does not**.
- SEBI preamble: **protect investors, develop and regulate** the securities market.
- MF schemes are benchmarked to a **Total Return Index (TRI)**, which includes dividends and interest, not a **Price Return Index**.
- Debt schemes carry a **Potential Risk Class (PRC) matrix**: **Macaulay duration** × **Credit Risk Value**; effective **1 Dec 2021**.
- **Segregated portfolio** separates distressed assets after a **credit event**; introduced after the **2018** credit crisis.
- **2008** crisis reforms: **no premature redemption in FMPs**; **"liquid plus"** name banned.
- RBI rules also apply to MFs for **money market, overseas investment, forex**.

### Investment restrictions

- Trades on **delivery basis** only; MFs **cannot give loans**.
- **No unlisted or privately placed** securities of **sponsor group**; listed group securities **≤25%** of net assets.
- Investing in other MF schemes: **≤5% of NAV**, **no fees**; **not for FoFs**.
- All schemes together: **≤10%** of a company's **voting capital** (or of one issuer's **REIT units**).
- Sponsor group can't hold **≥10%** in **another** MF's AMC or trustee company.
- Single issuer debt (investment grade): **≤10% of NAV**, up to **12%** with **trustee + AMC board** approval; **not** for G-secs, T-bills, triparty repo, **debt ETFs**.
- Unlisted NCDs: **≤10% of the debt portfolio**.
- Bank short-term deposits: **≤15%**, up to **20%** with **trustee** approval; **no management fee**; not in a bank that has **invested in the scheme**.
- Open-ended debt funds: **≥10% in liquid assets** (cash, G-secs, T-bills, G-sec repo); **not** for liquid and overnight funds.
- **NCPS** (non-convertible preference shares) = **debt**.
- Equity only if **listed or to be listed**; single company **≤10% of NAV** (**not** for index or sector funds).
- ELSS: **≥80%** equity.
- InvIT: MF across schemes **≤10%** of one issuer's units; scheme **≤10% of NAV** in InvITs, **≤5%** in one issuer.

### Advertisement code

- **No testimonials, no rankings, no celebrities, no projections**; no unfair comparisons.
- Standard warning: "**Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully.**" **No words added or removed**.
- Audio-visual: the **14-word** warning, visual + voice-over, **at least 5 seconds**.
- Vernacular ads carry the warning **in the vernacular**.
- Ad rules: **Sixth Schedule** of the MF Regulations.
- Dividend ads: **Rs per unit + face value + prevailing NAV**.
- Overnight, liquid, money market: **simple annualised** yield allowed if data for **7, 15 and 30 days** exists.
- Scheme **>3 years**: CAGR for **1, 3, 5 years and since inception** (since **allotment date**) + **point-to-point on Rs 10,000**; say **regular or direct**.
- **<6 months**: **no** performance; **6–12 months**: **simple annualised** 6-month return.
- Additional benchmark: equity, balanced, aggressive, BAF, multi-asset, retirement, children's = **Sensex/Nifty**; debt ≤1 yr + **arbitrage** = **1-yr T-bill**; other debt, **conservative hybrid, equity savings** = **10-yr GoI**.
- Also show all schemes of the **same fund manager** (CAGR 1/3/5 yrs); **>6 schemes**: count + **top 3 and bottom 3**.
- Celebrity endorsement: **industry level only**, **prior SEBI approval**, paid from the **investor education** pool; **not** for a scheme or AMC brand.
- **SIF**: needs a **distinct brand, logo, website/page**; MF brand allowed for **5 years** from SEBI approval.
- Unverified market news: forward only after the **Compliance Officer's** approval.

### Investor rights

- Proportionate **beneficial ownership**; can ask for a **unit certificate** or **demat**; demat units **freely transferable** (**except ELSS** in lock-in).
- Switch distributor or go direct by **written request**; **no NOC** needed.
- Can inspect: **Trust Deed, IMA, Custodial agreement, RTA agreement, AMC Memorandum & Articles**.
- **Up to 10 nominees**; no % given = **equal split**.
- Can **pledge** units.
- Complaint status goes in the **annual report**, MF site and **AMFI site**; pending complaints can **block new scheme launches**.
- Change in **fundamental attributes**: **≥30-day** exit at NAV with **no load**; letter + **English national daily + regional-language paper**.
- **75% of unitholders** can **remove the AMC** or **wind up** a scheme.
- Sue the **trustees** for breach of trust, **not the trust** (a notional entity).
- **Caveat emptor** applies; a **prospective investor** has no such rights.
- Unclaimed money **within 3 yrs**: paid at **prevailing NAV**; **after 3 yrs**: at **NAV at end of year 3**.
- Unclaimed amounts go to a **separate liquid plan**; max fee **0.50% p.a.**; **no exit load**.
- Debt trades disclosed **daily**, with a **15-day lag**.
- Illiquid security recovered later: substantial and **within 2 yrs** goes to **old investors**; otherwise to the **Investor Education Fund**.

### Grievances and codes

- AMC resolves complaints within **21 calendar days**.
- **SCORES** = SEBI's online complaints system; physical complaints get **scanned and uploaded**.
- Cyber framework **CSCRF** goals (from **CERT-In**): **Anticipate, Withstand, Contain, Recover, Evolve**.
- **Fifth Schedule** = SEBI code of conduct for **AMCs and trustees**; **AMFI Code of Ethics (ACE)** sets **higher, more explicit** standards.
- **AGNI** = AMFI Guidelines & Norms for **Intermediaries**.
- Breach process: explanation within **3 weeks** → **warning letter** → **2nd proven violation = ARN cancelled**, all AMCs told; **appeal to AMFI** allowed.

### MFD code (Appendix 3)

- MFD acts as a **fiduciary**: **investor's interest is paramount**.
- **No rebates or pass-back**, no gifts or inducements, **no churning**, **no splitting applications**.
- Disclose **all commissions** across competing schemes and **group/affiliate conflicts**.
- MFDs **cannot deal in direct plans**; platforms must say **regular plan with commission**.
- **No indicative portfolio, yield or assured returns**.
- Never enter the **MFD's own contact details** on a client form; always add the **EUIN**.
- Use **AMC-provided marketing material** only; no AMC logo without **written approval**.
- Suitability: ask about **financial status, experience, objectives**.
- Don't share client data with **group companies for cross-selling**.
- Sub-distributors must hold a **valid ARN**.
- **Refund clawback** commissions to AMCs.
- Report a Representative's **moral turpitude or arrest** to the **AMC and AMFI** immediately.
- Can't call yourself **adviser, wealth manager or consultant** unless a **SEBI RIA**.
- Tagline **"AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor"**, font **≥12** in print.

**Workbook sample answers:** 1-a (SEBI), 2-a (True), 3-c (80%), 4-a (industry-level celebrity), 5-d (ten).

## Chapter 5 – Scheme Related Information (10 marks)

### Mandatory documents

- **Scheme related documents** = **SID + SAI** (+ **KIM**); the standard warning refers to these.
- **Caveat emptor**: the investor is **presumed to have read** them; signing the form is **legal acceptance**.
- **SID** = details of **one scheme**; **SAI** = statutory info on the **MF/AMC**, **one SAI for all schemes**; legally **SAI is part of the SID**.
- Format **prescribed by SEBI**; content in the **same sequence**; MF may add other **material** disclosures.
- SEBI **does not approve**, it gives **observations**; documents are **vetted**, not approved.
- **Drafts** on **SEBI's** website; **final** documents on **AMFI's** website and the MF's own site.
- Offer = documents; **acceptance** = signed form + payment; **contract completes** on unit allotment.
- SID cover page: name, type (open/close/interval), **face value**, NFO dates, AMC and trustee details.
- Cover disclaimer: filed with SEBI with an AMC **Due Diligence Certificate**; **SEBI has not approved or certified** it.
- **Risk-o-meter and suitability statement** sit on the **front page of the SID**.
- Standard risk: **sponsor not liable** beyond its **initial contribution**; **past performance not indicative**.
- **Eligible and ineligible investors**: SID's **NFO and Ongoing Offer** section.
- Investment objective can be **primary + secondary**; also shown in **Highlights**.
- **How to apply** (PAN, KYC, payment modes, third-party rules, cut-off, minors, NRIs): **SAI**, read with SID.
- **Modes of transaction**: SAI's **"How to Apply"**.
- **Fees** hit the **NAV**; **loads** are paid **by the investor** at exit.

### Fundamental attributes

- (i) **Type of scheme**; (ii) **investment objective** + **min/max asset allocation**; (iii) **terms of issue**: liquidity, **aggregate fees and expenses**, **any safety net or guarantee**.
- To change them (**Reg 18(15A)**): letter to each unitholder, **English national daily + regional-language paper**, **30-day exit at NAV with no load**, trustees take **SEBI's comments**.

### SAI and KIM

- **SAI** covers constituents (sponsor, AMC, trustee, custodian, RTA, auditor, fund accountant if outsourced, bankers), **condensed financials** for schemes of the **last 3 FYs**, **valuation norms**, nomination, transmission, minors, pledge, tax, grievances.
- Investors can ask for a **printed SAI**; all SAIs on **AMFI's** site.
- **KIM** = summary of **SID + SAI**; **every application form must carry a KIM**.
- KIM shows performance over **1, 3, 5 years and since inception**.
- **Addendum** = interim change; it is **part of the documents** and **must go with the KIM**.

### Update timelines

- **SID**: first update within **6 months** of the half-year of launch; then within **1 month of each half-year** (**Sept and March** data).
- Change in fundamental attributes: **addendum immediately**; SID revised **after the exit window** (≥30 days); public notice.
- Other changes: **addendum** on website and to **distributors and ISCs**, attached to KIM/SID stock; **account statements show the load structure**.
- **SAI**: within **3 months of FY end**; material changes **ongoing**.
- **KIM**: at least **once every half-year**, within **1 month**; filed with SEBI **electronically**.

### Other disclosures

- Current value = **units × NAV**.
- NAV **every business day**, on the **AMC and AMFI** sites.
- **Liquid and overnight** NAVs also declared for **Sundays and holidays**; close-ended NAV **at least weekly**.
- **TER** disclosed **daily** on the AMC and AMFI sites.
- **Base TER increase**: email/SMS and website **≥3 working days before**; **decrease**: notify **immediately**.
- **Scheme dashboard** on the AMC site: AUM, TER, fund manager, benchmark, exit load, portfolio, performance.
- **Risk-o-meter**: **6 levels**: Low (**Irish Green**), Low to Moderate (**Chartreuse**), Moderate (**Neon Yellow**), Moderately High (**Caramel**), High (**Dark Orange**), Very High (**Red**).
- Risk-o-meter reviewed **monthly**; change = **notice-cum-addendum + email/SMS**; show the **scheme and benchmark** risk-o-meter together.
- Commodity risk score from **annualised volatility**, reviewed **quarterly** on **15 years** of prices: **<10% = 3**, **10–15% = 4**, **15–20% = 5**, **>20% = 6**.
- Risk-o-meter is only the **first step**, not the sole basis for decisions.
- **SIF Risk-Band**: **5 levels**; set at **NFO**; reviewed **monthly**; published within **10 days**; **31 March** level + number of changes on AMC and AMFI sites.
- **Portfolio**: **monthly and half-yearly**, with **ISIN**, within **10 days**, on AMC and AMFI sites.
- **Debt schemes**: portfolio **fortnightly**, within **5 days**, **plus yield**.
- **SIF portfolio**: **every alternate month** (May, Jul, Sep, Nov, Jan, Mar), within **10 days**; SIF **ISID** must include **scenario analysis**.
- **Unaudited half-yearly results** within **1 month** of 31 March / 30 Sept; ad in an **English + regional** paper.
- **Annual report** within **4 months** of FY end; ad in **English + Hindi** national dailies.

## Chapter 7 – NAV, TER and Pricing of Units (8 marks)

### Valuation

- Valuation norms: **Eighth Schedule**; goal = **fair treatment** of entering, exiting and staying investors.
- **10 fair valuation principles**; if they **conflict with the Valuation Guidelines**, the **Principles prevail**.
- Principle 1: value = **realisable value**, in **good faith**.
- Valuation policy approved by the **AMC Board**; a **new security type** needs its valuation method set **first**.
- Policy reviewed by an **independent auditor at least once a year**.
- Policy disclosed in the **SAI** and on the website.
- The **AMC** is responsible for **true and fair** valuation; it may **deviate** from policy (report to both **Boards**, disclose to investors).
- Valuation rationale kept for **audit trail** (**Reg 50**).
- Debt valuation uses trade prices from **all public platforms**.
- Traded equity: **last closing price** on the **principal exchange** (reasons in **writing**).
- Not traded that day: use **another exchange**, or the **earliest previous day within 30 days**.
- **No trade for 30 days = non-traded**: valued **in good faith** by AMC Board methods; reviewed by trustees; auditors report them **"fair and reasonable"**.
- Non-traded equity: **capitalisation of earnings** (P/E of comparables) ± book value, **less an illiquidity discount**.
- Convertibles: value the debt and equity parts **separately**.
- Warrants: **share value − exercise price − discount**.
- **Gold ETF**: **LBMA AM fixing**, USD per troy ounce, **995.0** fineness. **Silver ETF**: LBMA AM fixing, **999.0** fineness.

### NAV

- NAV = **net assets ÷ units outstanding** = **(total assets − liabilities other than to unitholders) ÷ units**.
- Workbook example: **Rs 217 cr ÷ 20 cr = Rs 10.85**.
- Net assets include **unrealised gains**.
- **Accrual principle**: income and expenses count when **due**, not when paid.
- Short-term borrowing for liquidity **≤ 6 months**.
- NAV rises with more **income and gains** and falls with more **expenses**.
- **Mark to market** stops dilution: issuing units **below NAV lowers** NAV for existing investors; redeeming **below NAV raises** it.

### Expenses and TER

- Recurring expenses (**allowed**): marketing incl. commission, brokerage, RTA, **trustee fees**, audit, custodian, investor communication, account statements, **insurance**, winding up, **statutory ads**, gold/silver **storage**, **rating fee** (capital protection), **listing fee**.
- Anything else is borne by the **AMC, trustee or sponsor**.
- **Equity-oriented** for TER = **≥65%** in equity (excluding index funds and ETFs).
- **Index fund / ETF**: TER **≤1.00%**.
- **FoF**: into liquid/index/ETF **1.00%**; **≥65%** into equity schemes **2.25%**; others **2.00%**; FoF's own layer **≤2×** the underlying's weighted TER.
- Open-ended TER slabs (equity / other):
  - first **Rs 500 cr**: **2.25 / 2.00**
  - next **250**: **2.00 / 1.75**
  - next **1,250**: **1.75 / 1.50**
  - next **3,000**: **1.60 / 1.35**
  - next **5,000**: **1.50 / 1.25**
  - next **40,000**: **−0.05% per Rs 5,000 cr**
  - balance: **1.05 / 0.80**
- **Close-ended / interval**: equity **1.25%**, others **1.00%**.
- Extra over TER: brokerage **≤0.12%** (cash) and **≤0.05%** (derivatives) of trade value.
- Extra **0.05%** of daily net assets allowed **only if the scheme has an exit load**.
- TER disclosed **daily** on the AMC and AMFI sites.

### AT1 and AT2 bonds

- Bank **perpetual AT1/AT2 bonds**: MF **≤10%** of one issuer's bonds; scheme **≤10%** of assets; **≤5%** in one issuer.
- Deemed maturity of **AT1** from **1 Apr 2023 = 100 years**; **AT2 = contractual maturity**.
- AT1 valued on **yield to call**; **close-ended debt** schemes **can't** buy perpetuals.

### Dividends (IDCW) and loads

- Paid **only from distributable surplus**: **valuation gains ignored**, **valuation losses deducted**.
- **Trustees** decide the amount and **record date**; monthly or more frequent payouts can be **delegated to AMC officials** (ratified at the **next board meeting**).
- Public notice **within 1 day** of the decision; record date **5 calendar days** from notice; notice says **NAV will fall**.
- **No notice** needed for **daily-to-monthly** payouts in liquid and debt schemes.
- **Late payment** = interest **from the record date**.
- Payout may include **capital** (from the **equalisation reserve**); the split must be disclosed.
- **Entry load banned**, so sale price = **NAV** (+ stamp duty).
- **Exit load**: repurchase price = **NAV × (1 − load)**; e.g. Rs 11 with 1% = **Rs 10.89**.
- Exit load: **no distinction by amount**; changes apply **only prospectively**; **none on bonus or IDCW-reinvestment units**; credited **back to the scheme**.
- Rs 25,000 at NAV Rs 43.21 = **578.570 units**.

### Accounting and segregated portfolio

- Scheme accounts **separate** from AMC accounts, with **different auditors**.
- NAV decimals: **4** for index, liquid and debt funds; **≥2** for equity and balanced funds.
- **Fractional units** allowed; exchanges may trade **whole units** only.
- **Segregated portfolio**: **no management fee**; other TER only **pro-rata on recovery**; NAV declared **daily**.
- Unrated debt can be segregated **only on actual default** (issuer with no rated debt).

**Workbook sample answers:** 1-a (SAI), 2-b (Rs 10.20), 3-a (1%), 4-c (distributable surplus).

## Chapter 8 – Taxation (4 marks)

- MF scheme income is **tax exempt** under **Sec 10(23D)**, because MFs are **trusts**.
- **Growth** option = **capital gains only**; **IDCW** option = dividend **+** capital gains.
- Tax depends on: **type of income**, **holding period**, **type of scheme**, **type of investor**, **purchase date** (**1 Apr 2023**) and **redemption date** (**23 Jul 2024**).
- **Equity-oriented** for tax = **>65%** in **listed Indian equity**.
- A **FoF investing in equity MFs** is **non-equity** for tax.
- Joint holding: income taxed in the **first holder's** hands.
- Holding periods: only **12 or 24 months**; **12 months** for **listed securities and equity funds**, **24 months** for others.
- **Grandfathering**: gains up to **31 Jan 2018** exempt; cost = **higher of actual cost or 31 Jan 2018 NAV**.
- Grandfathering example: cost Rs 1 lakh, 31 Jan 2018 value Rs 2 lakh; sold at **Rs 1.8 lakh = zero gain**; at **Rs 2.2 lakh = Rs 20,000** gain.
- Equity LTCG exempt up to **Rs 1.25 lakh** a year: Rs 90,000 gain = **no tax**; Rs 1.35 lakh = tax on **Rs 10,000**.
- Rates for redemptions **from 23 Jul 2024** (AMFI tax corner, not printed in the workbook): equity **STCG 20%**, equity **LTCG 12.5%** above Rs 1.25 lakh; debt funds bought **from 1 Apr 2023** taxed at **slab rate**.
- **DDT abolished** by Budget **Feb 2020**; dividends now taxed at the investor's **slab rate**; tax-exempt investors pay **nil**.
- IDCW **reduces NAV by more** than the investor's post-tax receipt.
- **Growth option is more tax-efficient** because of **tax deferral**.
- From **1 Apr 2021**, IDCW is split into **income distribution** and **capital distribution** for tax.
- **Stamp duty** (from **1 Jul 2020**): **0.005%** on **issue** (purchase, SIP, STP, switch-in, IDCW reinvestment); **0.015%** on **transfer**.
- Units are allotted on the amount **after stamp duty**.
- Capital loss **can't be set off against salary** or other income heads.
- **STCL** sets off against **STCG or LTCG**; **LTCL** only against **LTCG**.
- **Bonus stripping**: loss **disallowed** if bought **≤3 months before** the record date and sold **≤9 months after**; the loss becomes the **cost of the bonus units**.
- **STT** applies only to **equity-oriented funds**, on **sale or redemption**; **none on purchase**; **none on debt funds**.
- STT rates: redemption to the MF **0.001%**; delivery sale on exchange **0.001%**; non-delivery **0.025%**; all paid by the **seller**.
- **ELSS**: 80C up to **Rs 1.5 lakh** (individual/**HUF**), **Old Regime only**, **3-year lock-in**.
- ELSS lock-in runs from **each SIP instalment** and **each IDCW reinvestment**; the tax benefit goes to the **first holder**.
- Under the **new tax regime**, ELSS is just an **equity fund with a lock-in**.
- Some **old retirement funds** also give **80C**, with a **5-year lock-in**.
- **No TDS** on redemption for **residents**.
- **TDS on IDCW** for residents: **10%** if dividends exceed **Rs 5,000** a year.
- NRIs: TDS at the **lower of the Income Tax Act rate or the DTAA rate** (with documents).
- **GST on management fee** is charged **over and above** TER; GST on **other fees** and on **brokerage** sits **within** TER.
- **GST on exit load** is deducted **from the load** before crediting the scheme.
- **GST on distributor commission** can't be charged to the scheme.

**Workbook sample answers:** 1-b (MF income exempt), 2-a (multi-cap attracts STT).

## Chapter 9 – Investor Services (15 marks)

### NFO and plans

- Scheme idea comes from the **CIO** (objective) and **CMO** (market interest).
- **One scheme per category**; exceptions: **sector/thematic, index, FoF**.
- SID approved by the **trustees + AMC Board**, filed with SEBI, SEBI's observations incorporated.
- Open-ended NFO dates: **open, close, re-opening**; close-ended has **no re-opening date**.
- NFO open **min 3 working days**, **max 15 days** (**ELSS exempt** from the maximum).
- **Allot or refund within 5 business days** of NFO close; late refund = **15% p.a.** interest, **paid by the AMC**.
- Open-ended scheme **re-opens within 5 business days of allotment**.
- Realistic deployment timelines in the SID (**Reg 35(5)**).
- NFO price = **Rs 10** (no entry load): units = amount ÷ 10.
- **Demat credit within 2 working days**; SoA within **5 business days**.
- **ASBA**: money is only **blocked**, debited **on allotment**, keeps earning interest, **no refund wait**.
- **Trustees** can reject any application **without giving a reason**.
- **Direct plan**: **lower TER**, **no commission**, **separate NAV**; anything through a distributor = **Regular**.
- Only **two plans** (Direct and Regular) can differ by expense.
- Direct application: write **"Direct"** in the **ARN box**.
- IDCW **payout**: cash to bank, units **unchanged**, NAV falls (**ex-dividend** NAV); **cum-dividend** NAV = between announcement and payout.
- IDCW **reinvestment**: units added at **ex-dividend NAV**; e.g. Rs 2 × 100 units at NAV 20 = **10 units**.
- **Growth**: no dividend; NAV captures **all gains**.
- Workbook illustration: NAV 10 → 12, dividend **10% of face value = Re 1**; C reinvests at **Rs 11** = **9.09 units**; A = Rs 1,200, B = Rs 1,100 + Rs 100, C = Rs 1,200.
- Ongoing purchase: Rs 12,000 at Rs 12 = **1,000 units**.
- **Bonus 1:3** = 1 free unit per 3 held; NAV falls **proportionately**; **value unchanged**.
- **SIF**: **Rs 10 lakh** minimum at **PAN level**, across SIF strategies only.

### Account statements

- **SoA** issued each month with a transaction.
- Investors with **no transaction in 6 months**: statement **annually** (or email).
- **CAS monthly** if any transaction that month; **half-yearly CAS** if none; investors matched by **PAN**.
- "Transaction" includes purchase, redemption, switch, IDCW payout and reinvestment, SIP, SWP, STP, **bonus**.

### Eligible investors and the form

- Individuals: adults, up to **3 joint holders**.
- **Minors** invest via a guardian (**natural parent or court-appointed**); **no joint holders**.
- Minor's payment only from the **minor's, parent's/guardian's, or minor-guardian joint account**.
- **HUF** invests via the **Karta**.
- **NRI/PIO/OCI**: **repatriable** or **non-repatriable** basis.
- **QFI** must be from a **FATF** member and an **IOSCO MMOU** signatory.
- **FPIs**: **direct route** (**demat**) or **indirect route** (**UCR**, Unit Confirmation Receipt).
- Check **"Who can invest"** in the SID (e.g. some gilt plans only for PFs and trusts).
- The **first holder** gets all benefits (dividends, redemptions, tax).
- **PoA holder** can do everything **except nominate**; the grantor can still operate.
- Joint holders **can't be added or removed**, except a deletion on **death**.
- **KYC address overrides** the address on the application form.
- Form also collects **occupation, income/net worth, PEP status**; **FATCA/CRS** for foreign links.
- Bank details of the **first holder are mandatory**.
- Proof for a different payout account: cancelled cheque, passbook/statement **≤3 months old**, or bank letter.
- Demat applicants: details must **match the DP**; **DP's KYC is accepted**; payout goes to the **demat-linked bank account**.
- Nomination: **opt in or opt out** by signed declaration; **optional for joint folios**.

### Transactions and payments

- **Transaction slip** for additional purchases (folio details **override** the form).
- Redemption by **amount or units**; **FIFO**.
- If the balance falls below the minimum, the **whole folio** may be redeemed.
- **Demat redemption goes through the DP**.
- **Pool accounts of brokers discontinued**.
- **Two-factor authentication** mandatory for subscriptions and redemptions.
- SIF may have **different subscription and redemption frequencies**.
- **NACH** (by **NPCI**) replaced **ECS**.
- **UPI** uses a **VPA**; **AEPS** uses **Aadhaar + fingerprint**; **USSD \*99#** works without internet.
- **Credit cards not allowed**.
- **E-wallet** + **cash**: **Rs 50,000** per investor **per MF per FY** (umbrella limit).
- E-wallet money only if loaded via **cash, debit card or net banking**; **no third-party** payments.
- **Cash** only for **resident individuals, sole proprietors, minors** (KYC-compliant); payouts **only via bank**.
- **OTM** (One-Time Mandate) is at **folio level**, runs on NACH, frequency **"as and when presented"**, takes **3–4 weeks** to register.
- Cheques: **not post-dated** (except SIP), **not stale** (**>3 months**), **local or at-par**, first holder must be an account holder.
- NRI repatriable investment only from **NRE/FCNR** (with **FIRC**); non-repatriable also from **NRO**.
- **Third-party payment exceptions**: parent/grandparent/related person for a **minor** up to **Rs 50,000** per purchase or SIP instalment; **employer** payroll; **custodian for FPI**; **AMC paying distributor commission in units**.
- Third-party payer must be **KYC-compliant with PAN** + declaration; **DD against cash ≥ Rs 50,000 rejected**.
- **Not accepted**: stock-invests, postal/money orders, outstation cheques, post-dated cheques (except SIP), cash beyond the exception.
- Multiple bank accounts: **5** for individuals, **10** for non-individuals; first holder must be on each; one is **default**.
- A payout to an **unregistered** account goes to the **default** account.
- NRO-paid investment gets **NRO** payouts only; NRE-paid can go to **NRO or NRE**.
- **Instant Access Facility**: **liquid and overnight** schemes; **lower of Rs 50,000 or 90%** of value, **per day per scheme per investor**.

### Cut-off times

- **Equity and debt purchases (not liquid/overnight)**: **3:00 pm**; NAV of the day **funds are available** for use, **any amount**.
- **Liquid and overnight purchases**: **1:30 pm**; funds in before cut-off = NAV of the **day before** receipt; after cut-off = NAV of the **day before the next business day**.
- **Equity/debt redemption**: **3 pm**; **same day** NAV before, **next business day** after.
- **Liquid redemption**: **3 pm**; NAV of the **day before the next business day**.
- **Overnight redemption**: **3 pm**, **online 7 pm**.
- Cut-offs **don't apply** to **NFOs and international schemes**.
- Liquid funds calculate NAV for **every calendar day**.
- Time stamping is at the **OPoA** (not when the distributor receives the form); machines are **tamper-proof**; opening one is **reported to trustees**.
- Serial numbers **don't restart daily**; non-financial requests need only the **date**.
- Online time = **web server time**.

### KYC

- **Everyone** needs KYC: joint holders, NRIs, **PoA holder and issuer**, **guardian** (not the minor).
- **PAN exempt**: government and court officials, **Sikkim** residents, UN bodies, and **Micro-SIP + lump sum ≤ Rs 50,000** per year per MF (quote **PEKRN**).
- Micro-SIP ID proofs include a **photo debit card** (**not a credit card**).
- Address proof in the **spouse's name** is accepted.
- **KRA**: one KYC across the capital market; **IPV** mandatory (a bank's IPV is accepted).
- **CERSAI** = **Central KYC Records Registry** (notified **26 Nov 2015**); upload for individual accounts from **1 Aug 2016**; issues a **KYC Identifier**.
- **Aadhaar e-KYC** via **KUA / sub-KUA**.
- Online KYC via **eSign, DigiLocker, OTP, penny drop**.
- NRI KYC: **PAN**, passport/PIO/OCI, **overseas address**.
- Non-individuals: eligibility, **board resolution**, **authorised signatories**, **UBO**.
- UBO thresholds: **company >25%**; **partnership/body of individuals >15%**; **trust ≥15%** beneficiary + settlor + trustees; **not for listed companies**.
- **FATCA/CRS** status change reported **within 30 days**.

### Systematic transactions

- **SIP** = **rupee cost averaging**: average cost **below** the average NAV; can start **during an NFO**.
- **SWP** with 1% load at NAV 10: **101.0101 units** for Rs 1,000.
- **STP** = SWP from the **source** + SIP into the **target**; scheme minimums **don't apply**; loads and tax **do**.
- STP example: 0.25% load on Rs 1 lakh = **Rs 99,750** transferred.
- **DTP** (dividend transfer plan): needs the **IDCW option**; **overrides** payout/reinvest; invested the **next business day after the record date**; ends when source units = **nil**.
- Minor's SIP registered **only till majority**.
- SIP registration takes **15–30 days**: submit 5 Sept for a 30-day lead with SIP date 15th = first debit **15 Oct**.
- **SIP top-up**: +Rs 500 a year on Rs 2,500 = **3,000, then 3,500**; +10% = **2,750, then 3,025**.
- An SIP may be **cancelled** for **insufficient funds**.
- **Triggers** are **one-time**: stop-loss, appreciation, date or market-level.

### Non-financial transactions

- Nomination covers the **whole folio**; % must total **100**, else **equal**.
- Nomination only by **individuals**; **not** for a **minor's folio** or by a **PoA holder**; **all joint holders sign**.
- Nominee can be an **individual (even a minor or NRI)**, **government or local authority**, or a **religious/charitable trust**.
- **Not** a nominee: other trusts, society, company, firm, **Karta**, PoA holder.
- A new nomination **supersedes** the old one.
- Demat units follow the **demat account's nomination**.
- Nominee holds units **in trust for legal heirs**.
- An investor can let **one adult nominee** operate the folio if the investor is **physically incapacitated**.
- **Pledge**: all holders sign; units must be **out of lock-in**; can't sell until the **pledgee's NOC**; the lender can redeem **below the minimum**.
- **ISIN** for **each option**; demat to physical = **rematerialisation**.
- Name, address, PAN, contact changes go via the **KRA**; bank, mode of holding and nomination changes go to **each MF**.
- Change of guardian needs the **new guardian's KYC + NOC** from the old guardian.
- **Transmission**: first holder dies = **second holder** becomes first; single holder with nominee = **nominee**; joint holder **ranks above** nominee; no nominee = **legal heirs**.
- Transmission needs **KYC**, **death certificate**, **indemnity**.
- **Minor turns major**: folio **frozen** for the guardian; SIP/SWP/STP **stop**; submit the **MAM form** with PAN, KYC, bank proof, **banker-attested signature**, nomination, new SIP mandate.
- IDCW payout or reinvestment **continues** after majority.
- Minor's **demat** is single-name, so a **new demat account** is needed at majority.
- **NRI returns to India**: NRE/NRO/FCNR can't be operated; open a **resident account** (**RFC** for foreign currency) and a **new resident demat**; update the **KRA**.
- **Change of Karta**: death certificate, bank certificate, **indemnity from all co-parceners**.

### Turnaround times

- **Unit certificate within 5 working days**; **demat for close-ended within 2 working days**.
- SoA or demat credit within **5 working days** of NFO close or application.
- **Portfolio statement** within **10 days** of the half-year.
- **Annual report** within **4 months**.
- **Unaudited half-yearly results** within **1 month**.
- **Delay interest** on redemption or dividend is borne by the **AMC**, never the scheme.
- **MITRA** = a tracing platform for **inactive and unclaimed folios**, run by **CAMS and KFintech**.
- **DigiLocker** will hold MF statements and has its own **nominee** access.

## Chapter 10 – Risk, Return and Performance of Funds (7 marks)

### Risk factors

- The SID lists **standard (general)** and **specific** risk factors, plus **mitigation**.
- Standard statements: past performance **no guarantee**; scheme **name says nothing about quality**; sponsor not liable beyond its **initial contribution**.
- General risks: liquidity, interest rate, **reinvestment** ("interest on interest"), political, economic, **foreign currency** (for FPIs; the **AMC doesn't manage** it), settlement/counterparty, exchange-transaction.
- **Money market** securities are liquid but have **no well-developed secondary market**.
- **Securities lending**: borrower returns equivalent securities plus corporate benefits; risk = counterparty failure.
- **Short-selling** risks: counterparty and **illiquidity** of the borrowed stock.
- Moving down from **large to mid to small caps** increases volatility and liquidity risk.
- Derivatives are **leveraged**. Risk types: **counterparty** (the exchange guarantees settlement), **market liquidity**, **model** (mispricing), **basis** (hedge vs underlying).
- Debt risks: reinvestment, **rating migration** (AAA → AA+ = price falls), term structure, credit.
- Floaters: **spread risk** and **basis risk**.
- Repo in corporate bonds: counterparty risk, mitigated by **over-collateralisation**.
- Segregated portfolio: may be **illiquid**, may realise **nothing**, listing **doesn't ensure** liquidity.
- **Securitisation**: originator sells receivables to an **SPV (trust)**, which issues **PTCs**.
- Securitisation pool checks: **lower LTV = safer**; **seasoning** (more instalments already paid = better); **DPD** buckets.
- **Credit enhancement** lets PTCs be rated **above the originator**; they are **non-recourse**.
- Securitisation risks: **prepayment**, originator bankruptcy (**true sale**), **co-mingling**.
- MFs mostly buy securitised paper rated **AA and above**.
- **Systematic** = market = **non-diversifiable** (e.g. inflation). **Unsystematic** = company-specific = **diversifiable** (e.g. a strike).
- Investors are **rewarded only for systematic risk**.
- The fund manager can cut systematic risk only by **holding cash** (limited by SEBI).

### Drivers of return

- Equity: **security selection** and **market timing**.
- **Fundamental analysis** suits the **long term**; **technical analysis** (price-volume "**chartists**") suits the **short term and timing**.
- **EPS** = PAT ÷ shares; **P/E** = price ÷ EPS; **forward P/E** uses projected EPS.
- **PEG = 1** fair; **<1** undervalued; **>1** overvalued.
- **Book value/share** = net worth ÷ shares; **P/BV** = price ÷ BV.
- **Dividend yield** = DPS ÷ price; it **falls in bull markets**, **rises in bear markets**.
- **Growth** stocks: **high P/E and PEG, low dividend yield**; fall more in corrections.
- **Value** stocks: priced **below intrinsic value**; need a **long horizon**.
- High **valuation** ≠ high **price**.
- **EIC** framework: **Economy, Industry, Company**.
- **Top-down**: economy first, avoids a bad **sector**. **Bottom-up**: stock first, sector allocation is the **result**.
- Debt returns are driven by **interest rates and credit spreads**.
- **T-bills**: issued by **RBI** for the GoI. **CDs**: banks **7 days–1 year**, FIs **1–3 years**. **CP**: companies, **up to 1 year**.
- Government issues **bonds**; private companies issue **debentures**.
- **Credit spread** = corporate yield − **gilt** yield; rating agencies: **CRISIL, ICRA, CARE, India Ratings (Fitch)**.
- **YTM** = return if **held to maturity**; **HPR** = interest + capital gain or loss.
- Floater coupon = **base + spread**; it **holds its value** when rates move.
- Rates **expected up**: shorten duration or buy floaters. Rates **expected down**: buy **long** duration.
- A 9.5% AAA bond when market yield is 8.5% trades at a **premium**.
- Debt fund returns hinge on **interest-rate calls**; equity funds on **sector and stock** calls.
- Liquid, ultra-short and floater funds focus on **accrual**.
- **Gold** returns = **global price + rupee** (**weaker rupee = higher** return) + duties; gold funds are **passive**.
- **Real estate**: a **local** asset; driven by economy, infrastructure, rates; **no MF real estate schemes** currently.

### Returns

- **Simple** return: 12 → 15 = **25%**; 120 → 135 = **12.5%**.
- **Annualised** = simple × 12 ÷ months: 5% in 6 months = **10%**; 3% in 4 months = **9%**.

<p class="formula">CAGR = (Later value ÷ Initial value)<sup>1/n</sup> − 1</p>

- Rs 1,000 to Rs 4,000 in 2 yrs = **100%** CAGR.
- Rs 10,000 at 10% for 3 yrs = **Rs 13,310** (vs **13,000** simple).
- n = **days ÷ 365**.
- With dividends, SEBI uses the **reinvestment method** (reinvest at **ex-dividend NAV**): workbook example **1,000 → 1,080 → 1,152 units**, value **Rs 17,280**, **43.65%** over **1.51 years**.
- **Investor return ≠ scheme return** because of **loads**, **taxes** and **cash-flow timing**; advertised returns **ignore loads**.
- Holding periods: **<1 year = absolute**, **>1 year = CAGR**.
- **Rolling returns** = average of many consecutive periods; removes **start/end-date bias**.
- **No promised returns** except **assured-return schemes**, which need a named **guarantor** in the SID.

### Risk from the investor's view

- "**Low risk ≠ zero risk**": liquid and ultra-short funds have faced **credit events**.
- Without segregation, redemptions **raise** the bad paper's share: 5% becomes **10%** after 50% redemptions.
- Arbitrage funds using **paired arbitrage, alpha hedging, merger arbitrage** take views, so they're **not risk-free**.
- Schemes being **wound up** are **listed** (**Reg 39(2)(a)**).
- Real estate funds are **high risk** but **less risky than direct property**.

### Risk measures

- **Variance**: fluctuation vs **own average**; workbook example **0.67 vs 39.58**.
- **SD** = **√variance** = **total risk**; for **debt and equity**; needs **≥30 observations**.
- Annualise SD: **×√52** weekly (0.65 → **4.70%**), **×√12** monthly, **×√252** daily.
- **Beta** (from **CAPM**) = **systematic** risk vs a diversified index (**β = 1**); **equity only**.
- Beta 0.8 moves **8%** when the market moves 10%; beta 1.2 moves **12%**.
- **Modified duration** = interest rate sensitivity; the **professional's** measure.
- **WAM** example: 70% × 4 yrs + 30% × 1 yr = **3.1 years**; simpler, used with **lay investors**.
- **Credit rating** profile = default risk; **G-secs and cash have no credit risk**.

### Credit events, gating and side-pocketing

- **Credit event** = **default, delay or downgrade**.
- Rs 10,000 cr scheme with 8% in one paper; 20% redemptions push exposure to **10%**.
- **Gating** (restricting redemptions): only in **market-wide** crises (liquidity, **exchange closure**, operational **force majeure**); **never for one bad security**.
- Gating: **max 10 working days in any 90 days**; needs **AMC Board + trustee** approval; **SEBI informed immediately**.
- During gating, the **first Rs 2 lakh** of every redemption is paid normally.
- **Segregated portfolio** (Dec **2018**): **optional**; triggered by a **downgrade**. Unrated debt can be segregated **only on actual default**, after AMFI informs all AMCs (**7 Nov 2019**).
- New schemes after **7 Nov 2019** must carry **enabling provisions** in the SID.
- Segregation policy approved by **trustees**; effective from the **credit-event day**; **press release** issued.
- Investors get **equal units** in the segregated portfolio; **no subscription or redemption** in it; **listed within 10 working days**.
- New investors get **main-portfolio units only**.
- Segregated portfolio costs: **no management fee**; TER only **on recovery**, capped at the **main portfolio's average**; legal costs within TER, **excess borne by the AMC**; **never charged to the main portfolio**.
- Workbook example: total NAV **1,000.1227** = main **876.2427** + segregated **123.88**.
- Mitigants: **longer holding** (**10 yrs = almost no negative returns** historically); **match holding period to portfolio maturity**.

**Workbook sample answers:** 1-a (True), 2-c (technical), 3-c (12.5%), 4-a (variance).

## Chapter 11 – Mutual Fund Scheme Performance (7 marks)

### Benchmarks

- A benchmark must match the scheme's **investment objective, asset allocation and investment strategy**, and be run by an **independent agency**, transparently.
- **Past returns are never** a basis for choosing a benchmark.
- An index fund's benchmark = **the index it tracks**.
- Benchmark is chosen by the **AMC with the trustees**, stated in the **SID**; a change must be **justified and documented**.
- **TRI since 1 Feb 2018** (was PRI); TRI adds **dividends/interest**.
- TRI makes it look like **fewer schemes beat** the benchmark; the PRI–TRI gap = index dividend yield, **\~1.5–2.5%**.
- Periods before TRI existed use a **composite CAGR** (PRI then TRI).
- Equity benchmark depends on **scheme type** (diversified vs **sectoral**, e.g. **Bankex**), **universe** (large vs mid cap), **concentration** (fewer stocks = **Sensex 30 / Nifty 50**; more = Nifty 100/200/500).
- Debt indices come from **CRISIL, ICICI Securities, NSE**, recommended by **AMFI**.
- **I-Bex** sub-indices: **Si-Bex 1–3 yrs**, **Mi-Bex 3–7 yrs**, **Li-Bex >7 yrs**.
- CRISIL indices, e.g. **Liquid Fund Index** for liquid funds, **Composite Bond Fund Index** for **dynamic bond** funds.
- Hybrid benchmark = **blend**, e.g. **65% Sensex + 35% I-Bex**.
- CRISIL hybrid indices: **25+75 Aggressive**, **50+50 Moderate** (balanced), **75+25 Conservative**; equity leg = **S&P BSE 200 TRI**.
- **Gold ETF** benchmark = **price of gold**; international funds use a local index (e.g. **Shanghai Composite**, **S&P 500**).
- **Two-tier benchmarks** (SEBI **Oct 2021**, effective **1 Jan 2022**): Tier 1 = **category**, Tier 2 = **fund manager's style**; applies to **debt and equity** schemes; all must be **TRI**.
- **Single benchmark** for **hybrid/solution, sectoral, index/ETF** schemes.
- **FoF**: a single-fund FoF uses the **underlying's** benchmark; a multi-fund FoF uses a **broad market index**.
- Debt Tier-1 benchmarks follow **PRC**, from **1 Apr 2022**.

### Performance measures

- **Relative return** vs benchmark or peers **ignores risk**.
- **AMCs and trustees** review relative returns **periodically**.

<p class="formula">Sharpe = (R<sub>s</sub> − R<sub>f</sub>) ÷ SD &nbsp;·&nbsp; Treynor = (R<sub>s</sub> − R<sub>f</sub>) ÷ Beta &nbsp;·&nbsp; IR = (R<sub>p</sub> − R<sub>b</sub>) ÷ SD of excess return</p>

- R<sub>s</sub> − R<sub>f</sub> = **risk premium**; the risk-free rate = **T-bill** return.
- **Sharpe** uses **SD** (total risk). Example: (7 − 5) ÷ 0.5 = **4**. Higher = better; compare **like with like** (never equity vs debt).
- **Treynor** uses **beta** (systematic risk). Example: (8 − 5) ÷ 1.2 = **2.5**. For **diversified equity**.
- **Alpha** = actual − **beta-expected (optimal)** return; **positive = outperformance**; for diversified equity.
- **Information Ratio** = **excess return over the Tier-1 benchmark ÷ SD of excess return** (daily data).
- IR is the **only mandatory** risk-adjusted measure; disclosed **daily** on the AMC and AMFI sites (**Jan 2025** circular).
- **Tracking error** = **SD of excess returns**; it shows how closely an **index fund** tracks its index (**ideally zero**) and how **consistently** an active fund beats its benchmark (**lower = more consistent**).
- Risk-adjusted **"calculations are arithmetic; evaluation is an art"**.

### Disclosure sources

- **SID** updates performance **once a year**; the **factsheet** is **monthly** but **not mandatory**, and ad rules apply to it.
- Product label shows the **objective** (wealth creation / income / liquidity), **asset class**, **holding period** and **risk-o-meter**.
- **Discrete annual returns** show **consistency** across market conditions.
- Factsheet macro readings: high **fiscal deficit** pushes **interest rates up**; **inflation** above comfort brings **monetary tightening**; low **global rates** pull **flows into emerging markets**; **oil** prices move the **currency**.
- **AMFI website** has performance data for **all schemes**; data vendors include **Morningstar and Value Research**.

**Workbook sample answers:** 1-d (Sharpe), 2-d (past returns), 3-a (TRI), 4-b (tracking error).

## Chapter 12 – Mutual Fund Scheme Selection (15 marks)

### Investor fit

- Step 1: **set goals**; step 2: **assess situation and risk appetite** (**need + ability + willingness**).
- Needs map to funds: **growth → equity**, **income → debt**, **parking → liquid**.
- Equity needs a **long horizon**, even for an investor willing to take risk.
- **Age alone is a poor guide**; use the **time horizon to each goal**.
- **Core** = long-term goals (diversified, large, mid cap). **Satellite** = tactical: **sector funds**, **long gilt** (rates expected down), **gold** (high inflation or uncertainty).
- Workbook illustration: a moderate investor at **80% core / 20% satellite**.

### Risk hierarchies (left to right)

- Overall, **risk and return rise**: liquid → debt → hybrid → equity.
- **Interest rate risk rises**: overnight → liquid → ultra-short → low → short → medium → medium-long → long.
- **Credit risk rises**: **gilt** → banking & PSU → corporate bond → **credit risk fund**.
- **Equity risk falls** in this order: small → mid → multi → large & mid → **large** (lowest).
- **Concentration risk rises**: diversified → **focused** → thematic → **sector**.
- **Hybrid risk rises**: **arbitrage** → equity savings → conservative → dynamic asset allocation → multi-asset → balanced → **aggressive**.
- Product labelling history: **2013**: 3 colours (**Blue** = low, **Yellow** = medium, **Brown** = high); **Apr 2015**: **5 levels** + **Riskometer**; now **6 levels** (Ch 5).

### Choosing by strategy

- Low-risk equity: **index or value** fund. Extra risk for extra return: **growth** fund.
- **Passive** = **no selection risk**, lower cost, but **NAV still falls** when the index falls.
- **Active** = higher cost and risk, and must **beat the benchmark** to justify the choice.
- Open-ended: liquidity at **NAV less load**, but must hold some **cash** (dilutes returns) and faces **flow pressure**.
- Close-ended: exit only via the exchange, often at a **discount with no limit**; price **converges to NAV near maturity**.
- Close-ended funds can hold **illiquid or long-gestation** stocks; choose them only if the money can stay **locked in**.
- Focused funds are **riskier than diversified** but **less risky than sector** funds.
- **Sector** funds depend on **timing**, so they belong in the **satellite** portfolio.
- **Thematic** funds (MNC, PSU, infra) sit **between diversified and sector**.
- "**Diversification is protection against ignorance.**"
- Mid/small caps shine in **recoveries**, when large caps turn expensive, but many **fail in downturns**.
- **Growth** outperforms in **bull** markets; **value** outperforms in **falling** markets and needs a **long horizon**.
- **International funds** = foreign equity + **currency**: a **stronger dollar helps** an Indian investor; they suit the **satellite** portfolio.
- **FMP**: horizon must **match maturity**, gives more **predictable** returns than FDs, check **credit risk**; exit before maturity **only via exchange**.
- **TMFs** are replacing FMPs: G-secs, SDLs, **AAA PSU** paper; **liquid** via the AMC.
- **Short duration (1–3 yrs)**: use when rates are **expected to rise**; low volatility; core for low-risk investors.
- **Liquid fund**: lowest risk, compares to a **savings account**, for parking **up to 91 days**; using it longer **wastes return**.
- **Floater** NAV stays **steady**.
- **Duration-managed** debt funds are **more volatile**.
- Hybrid funds: judge the **equity quality** and the **debt credit/duration** separately; "**conservative" refers only to equity allocation**.
- Be **cautious with dynamic asset allocation** funds.
- A balanced fund's tax status depends on its **equity share**.
- **Gold ETF** tracks the **gold price**. A **gold sector fund** holds **mining shares**, which rise and fall **more** and move with company factors.

### Choosing within a category

- Check the portfolio is **true to label** (e.g. a "value" fund full of expensive front-line stocks isn't).
- **Performance**: **consistent** outperformance, **downside protection**, vs **benchmark and peers**, over **rolling** periods.
- Look-back: **equity ≥ 5 yrs**; **long-term debt ≥ 3 yrs**; **short-term debt** shorter; **liquid funds** use **7 days, 15 days, 1 month**.
- **Fund age** matters most where scheme returns **diverge** (e.g. **multi cap** more than large cap).
- **Fund size**: large helps **large-cap** funds; hurts **mid-cap or sector** funds with limited stocks.
- **Portfolio turnover** = (purchases + sales) ÷ average net assets: **10,000 ÷ 5,000 = 200%**, average holding **6 months**.
- Judge turnover against **style**: 6 months is too short for **value**, fine for **momentum**.
- **Costs** matter most in **debt** and **index** funds.
- Star ratings = **past performance**, **no predictive** power; this quarter's best fund is rarely next quarter's.
- Target the **top few** consistently: **top 3–5** in small categories, **top 10–15%** in big ones.

### Options and do's and don'ts

- **IDCW payout** gives cash flow but is **not assured** (needs distributable surplus); taxed at **slab**.
- For regular income, **SWP is better** than IDCW (capital gains tax, plus **STT** on equity).
- IDCW suits only **tax-exempt** investors; **growth** compounds **pre-tax**.
- Book gains or losses to **set off** against others.
- Do: ensure **suitability** (**PFUTP**), stick to **asset allocation**, understand **objective and strategy**, watch **taxes and loads** on redemption, keep a **written, consistent** selection method.
- Don't: **chase past performance**.

**Workbook sample answers:** 1-d (gilt), 2-a (risk-o-meter), 3-b (False), 4-a (multi-cap), 5-a (FMPs), 6-a (True), 7-c (both).
